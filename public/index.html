Frequently asked

Before you take the next step.

Is Dentist Guide Vietnam a dental clinic?

This design is for a dental information and patient coordination platform. Treatment decisions and clinical care must be provided by the treating licensed clinic and its clinicians.

Can I receive a final treatment plan online?

An online review can provide preliminary information. The treating dentist must confirm the diagnosis, suitability, treatment plan and costs after the required examination.

How many visits should I plan?

Ask your treating clinic for a written schedule. Some treatments require multiple visits or separate trips, and timelines depend on individual assessment and healing.

What should a quote include?

Ask for the treatment scope, materials, clinician, provisional and final restorations, exclusions, payment terms, follow-up arrangements and any written warranty conditions.
